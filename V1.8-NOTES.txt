Fixes:
1. Student/academician profile project cards now use a stable responsive layout.
2. Project links and timestamps are placed in a dedicated non-overlapping footer.

Important SMTP values:
MAIL_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
MAIL_PORT=587
MAIL_USERNAME=your-address@gmail.com
MAIL_PASSWORD=your-16-character-app-password
MAIL_FROM=your-address@gmail.com
MAIL_SMTP_AUTH=true
MAIL_STARTTLS=true
